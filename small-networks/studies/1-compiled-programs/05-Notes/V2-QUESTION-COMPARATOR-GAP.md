# V2 question / comparator / gap / proceed decision

29 September 2026. This supersedes the pilot's closure decision. Source access was restored through the approved network route; a sandbox DNS failure was not a research feasibility result.

**Question.** For a fixed claim about independently replacing two named internal program variables, how often do finite validation panels accept reference-inconsistent alignments? Does joint testing improve on an equal state-access budget of full-domain single-variable testing? Can a divergence diagnostic distinguish harmless nuisance changes from harmful changes?

**Already known.** Grant et al. show misleading successful interventions and distinguish harmful divergence from readout-null divergence. Their Appendix A.3 motivates a transported-divergence control. Méloux et al.'s CAE explicitly tests unmapped variables and multiple intervention cardinalities. Geiger et al.'s abstraction framework already supplies compositional intervention semantics. Chen et al. demonstrate multiple task-supporting circuits; Rai et al. show dataset-specific discovery and explicitly scoped circuits. None of these makes low structural overlap an invalidity label. The existence of the failure is not our novelty claim.

**Qualified reproduction.** The unmodified Grant repository's section 4.2 sanity script passes every assertion at commit f2548d2ea9b4f4b87a87ba5d53db43838d15c521, Apache-2.0. The CAE controlled XOR experiment also ran at d91f65b5620423d3eae98478bc9843f4a40e361a, MIT. Its nominally valid reference has nonzero error because the implementation ignores mediator/output interventions; this setting fails qualification. A separately labelled root-only adaptation gives zero error for the valid reference and nonzero faithfulness-aware error for the backup system. Neither a failed gate nor that adaptation is presented as an exact successful replication of all published CAE settings.

**Narrow contribution.** A transparent, finite, cost-accounted validation comparison on compiled networks with frozen learned and control candidate procedures, independently checked carrier laws, and complete program-level records. This is a small controlled benchmark and a reproduction/validation study; no priority claim for a new method or universal certificate is made. The most informative result may be that ordinary tests with better coverage already suffice.

**Proceed.** Tracr at 9ce2b8c82b6ba10e62e86cf6f390e7536d4fd2cd qualifies in the isolated CPU environment. Four categorical lookup programs passed before four numerical additive programs were attempted and qualified. An independently implemented NumPy forward agrees with official JAX execution on every residual and all 27 legal inputs. Exact native programs, full-support mean-contrast alignments, restricted-support mean alignments, and an orthogonal DAS adaptation provide both reference classes. The final experiment can proceed without a lighter substitute, larger model, third family or GPU.

**Scope warning.** The reference claim names the compiler's effective A/B carrier codes and allows changes in a proven readout-null nuisance space. This is stronger than output equivalence and narrower than every possible explanation of a network. A reference-inconsistent alignment may still satisfy a weaker output claim. The oracle and assays share the high-level counterfactual definition, but labels never inspect sampled/joint suffix outputs.

**Adaptations.** The primary panel's complement resampling is CAE-inspired, not the released CAE metric. Its transported divergence uses an exact legal counterfactual in place of an estimated class manifold and randomly samples transport contexts; it is inspired by Grant Appendix A.3, not an implementation of counterfactual-latent training or the complete paper's training experiments. Raw latent distance is a deliberately naive comparator, not attributed to Grant as their recommended validity test. OASR/DCD are inspected comparators on circuit discovery and scope, not competing classifiers for the present variable-alignment claim.
